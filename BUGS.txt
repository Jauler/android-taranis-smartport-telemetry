Known bugs
==========


1. Takeoff point is lost on reconnect, decoder restart or screen rotation mid-flight
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Area: terrain readouts (height above ground, altitude limit)
Files: manager/TerrainMonitor.kt, ui/MapsActivity.kt

What happens:
  The height above ground is computed relative to the ground at the takeoff
  point. When, during a flight, the telemetry link drops and reconnects, the
  decoder restarts, a replay is seeked, or the screen is rotated, the takeoff
  point is forgotten and the next GPS position, taken while the aircraft is in
  the air, becomes the new takeoff point. A takeoff point set by hand ("Set
  takeoff point at UAV location") is lost as well.

Why:
  - onConnected() and onDecoderRestart() in MapsActivity call
    terrainMonitor.reset(), which clears `takeoff` and `takeoffLocked`.
  - TerrainMonitor lives in the Activity and is created in onCreate(), so a
    rotation (no configChanges in the manifest) starts a fresh monitor with
    no takeoff point. DataService keeps the connection and the track, but
    onServiceConnected() does not hand the track's first point to the new
    monitor.

Example:
  Takeoff from a field at 100 m above sea level. The aircraft flies at 100 m
  (relative) over a hill whose ground is at 160 m, then the link drops and
  comes back. The hilltop becomes the takeoff point:
    shown AGL = 100 + 160 - 160 = 100 m, true AGL = 100 + 100 - 160 = 40 m.
  Back over the valley (ground 60 m) it shows 200 m: a false "height limit
  exceeded". Over lower ground the error goes the other way and the limit
  warning is suppressed.

Suggested fix:
  Keep the takeoff point (and the manual lock) for the whole session: across
  reconnects, decoder restarts and activity recreation, e.g. hold it in
  DataService or seed a new monitor from dataService.points.first() and the
  armed state in onServiceConnected(). Clear it only when a new session starts
  (idle state, new replay).
